What happens beneath your skin and hair

Depth ≈ 0.0 mm

Under the skin

Surface

What you see.

Acne marks, tan, dullness and uneven tone. This is where most people start looking, and it is only the top of the story.

My approach: cosmetology and a routine matched to your skin.

Epidermis

Where tone and texture begin.

Pigment and the skin's protective barrier sit in this thin outer layer. Dark patches, rough texture and sun damage start here.

My approach: laser skin aesthetics and Ayurvedic topical care.

Dermis

Where skin heals, ages and flares.

Collagen, blood vessels and oil glands live here. Acne, scars, ageing and recurring flare-ups trace back to this layer, and Ayurveda also reads them as signals from digestion, stress and sleep.

My approach: Ayurvedic dermatology for the cause, aesthetics for the result.

Hair follicle

The root of hair health.

Each hair grows from a follicle about 3 to 4 mm below the scalp. Hair fall, thinning and dandruff are worked out at the root, so I start with the scalp.

My approach: trichology, scalp care and Ayurvedic treatment.

02 · Conditions

From everyday breakouts to long-standing skin disease.

I assess and manage these conditions with Ayurvedic dermatology, and with aesthetic treatment where it helps. The classical Ayurvedic name is shown where one applies. Results differ from person to person, and some conditions need care over time.

Eczema

Vicharchika

Dry, itchy, inflamed patches that flare and settle. Triggers, stress and diet are looked at together.

Psoriasis

Ekakushtha · Kitibha

Thick, scaly plaques with a relapsing course. Needs long-term care and steady follow-up.

Hives

Sheetapitta

Sudden itchy wheals that come and go, often linked to a trigger.

Contact dermatitis

Rashes from products, metals, plants or fabrics. Finding the trigger comes first.

Seborrheic dermatitis

Red, flaky, oily areas on the scalp, face and chest.

Acne

Yuvanapidika

Whiteheads, blackheads and inflamed pimples, treated at the cause and in the routine.

Acne scars and marks

Textured and pigmented marks left behind. Laser skin aesthetics where suitable.

Oily, congested skin

Clogged pores, shine and blackheads.

Melasma

Vyanga

Brown-grey patches on the cheeks and forehead, often hormonal and sun-sensitive.

Vitiligo

Shwitra

White patches where pigment is lost. Long-term care and regular monitoring.

Post-acne marks and tan

Dark marks left by breakouts, and sun tan that will not fade.

Fungal infections, ringworm

Dadru

Itchy, ring-shaped patches that spread if left untreated.

Pityriasis versicolor

Sidhma

Light or brown scaly spots, common in warm, humid weather.

Scabies

Pama

Intense itching, worse at night. Household members are treated together.

Boils and folliculitis

Painful, pus-filled bumps around hair follicles.

Hair fall and thinning

Khalitya

Excess shedding or thinning, traced to the scalp, nutrition, stress or a family pattern.

Alopecia areata

Indralupta

Smooth, round patches of hair loss. Follow-up and patience matter.

Dandruff

Darunaka

Flaking and itch on the scalp, treated at the scalp and in the routine.

Warts

Charmakila

Rough, raised growths caused by a virus.

Corns

Kadara

Hard, thickened skin on pressure points of the feet.

Long-standing conditions need patience and follow-up, and results vary from person to person. If a condition needs another specialist or urgent care, I will say so.
